#### New Mission Types



###### **\*\*\*NOTE: Do not read this document if you want to undertake the missions without hints and tips before you play.\*\*\***



1. ##### Combat Patrol

###### Mission Overview

Combat Patrol sees you on a time limited run, where you will face a series of foes and targets, while also being harassed from afar. The mission seeks to maintain constant tension through the timer requirements while also forcing you to balance quick timer completion with destruction of targets. Not all targets are Primary Objectives, though all timer locations are.



###### Mission Length

Moderate. The need to move quickly, with new objectives popping up, has you move through the mission with speed.



###### Lance Composition Advisory

You don't need blinding speed for this mission, but moving too slow will fail. You can accomplish the mission with 4 ‘Mechs, but it will be hard.



###### Opposition Notes

Your foe is spread out but can mass if you make the wrong calls. They are not overly heavy and have mobility, plus significant support.



###### Designer Thoughts

This was the first of the new missions that were designed and was something of a roof of concept. That said, the need to balance time and objectives while getting harassed the whole way makes for a tense experience. I enjoy this mission shape, as it forces you not to dwell on any one decision, but to think on your feet as the mission pressures change.



##### 2\. Major Push

###### Mission Overview

Major push is designed to simulate a large advance, with allied support, against multiple enemy installations. Though the mission feels easy enough to begin with, the pressure slowly mounts, with more foes arriving, more often and the effect of damage and declining ammunition loads becoming critical towards the end of the mission. The biggest rewards have the biggest risk, coming late in the battle and can be worth the effort if you succeed, or bring you to ruin if you do not.



###### Mission Length

Long. This is a multi-phase, major engagement, with a large risk/reward element and the opportunity to bug out if things get too tough.



###### Lance Composition Advisory

This mission requires more ‘Mechs than a single lance can provide, with both fast, and durable BattleMechs having roles to play. Being able to cycle ‘Mechs or eject pilots will become necessary late in the mission, so redundancy and using your allied lance carefully will be essential. The command Abilities mod can assist here with directing your allies appropriately.



###### Opposition Notes

Your foe is big, bad and really pissed off you are in the area. Speed helps here, sort of. The longer you take, the more ‘Mechs turn up, but the same is true of speed as you activate more foes by taking targets.



###### Designer Thoughts

This mission was designed to simulate an advance on a core enemy area. Inspired by Warzone, I wanted to create a mission where tension builds, but waves or enemy units are not dictated by well spread spawn points, but by time and your accomplishments, making the mission tension rise as more enemies react to the advance into their territory.



##### 3\. Rapid Advance

###### Mission Overview

Rapid Advance seeks to simulate an assault with an allied unit against and enemy line, where you strike, push through and try to occupy a location in their rear rapidly. This is a mission where using the commander abilities to manoeuvre your allies into position can be very useful. Landing with a single lance will make the mission a real challenge, as you need two lances (one of yours and your allied lance) to pin the enemy company, while a third breaks for the target.



###### Mission Length

Moderate.



###### Lance Composition Advisory

A heavy lance to back your ally, along with a good cavalry lance are a useful mix for this mission.



###### Opposition Notes

The opposition is right in your face from the get-go, so be ready for a lot of fire early.



###### Designer Thoughts

Another mission mixing time pressures and a strong foe, Rapid Advance seeks to test your ability to force a breakthrough and then hold an objective against a determined foe. The temptation of this mission is getting your forces entangled with the initial defensive line, but maintaining the balance between the true objective, without leaving to many foes in your rear, is the key.



##### 4\. Planetary Landing

###### Mission Overview

Planetary Landing places you on the ground defending a fixed position, while also having to cover arriving allies who move to their exists. Your job is to defend the landing zone and the waves of allies who are arriving in random order, thereby simulating the tension of a landing zone. This mission places a huge premium on risk/reward thinking, tactical positioning and force division to accomplish all of your primary goals.



###### Mission Length

Long. Multiple escort waves and enemy attacks occur on multiple axis.



###### Lance Composition Advisory

You need numbers, speed, brawn and armour.



###### Opposition Notes

All sorts, from all directions. Each convoy is attacked, and there are roving enemy lances as well.



###### Designer Thoughts

This mission was designed to simulate a botched landing, where allied lances are dropping in and moving out under fire, as you cover them. Making sure everyone gets where they need to be safely is not easy, and folks will get left behind on this most chaotic of battlefields.



##### 5\. Planetary Evacuation

###### Mission Overview

Planetary evacuation is essentially Planetary Invasion in reverse. Your task here is to escort incoming waves of allies to the evac zone, while the enemy tries to hammer you as you defend a fixed area. Like your task in Planetary Landing, your job is to defend the landing zone as the waves of allies who are arriving in random order at random points make their way to you, thereby simulating the tension of an evacuation zone under fire.



###### Mission Length

Long. Multiple escort waves and enemy attacks occur on multiple axes.



###### Lance Composition Advisory

You need numbers, speed, brawn and armour.



###### Opposition Notes

All sorts, from all directions. Each convoy is attacked, and there are roving enemy lances as well.



###### Designer Thoughts

The opposite of the landing is, in some ways, easier, as you are defending one point as convoys come to you, though in some instances, they are coming in very hot and you cannot simply sit in  bunker and lob fire. Terrain and positioning is even more important here, and your allies will do everything possible to make life hard on you.



##### 6\. Complex Assignment

###### Mission Overview

Complex assignment sends you across a map undertaking different assignments and is meant to be an unpredictable mixed bag of mission objectives. Your force mix will need to be flexible, as will your selection of secondary objectives, as you do not know what awaits you on the mission.



###### Mission Length

Moderate



###### Lance Composition Advisory

You don’t need to take down Kerensky, so a balanced lance that can operate well together against multiple threats is advised.



###### Opposition Notes

Each objective it not heavily defended, but there enough foes to ware you down if you take anyone too lightly.



###### Designer Thoughts

Though it is similar to Combat Patrol, complex assignment lacks the timers and pure location focus objective set of the Combat Patrol. Where the former is timed and you can ignore foes, in Complex Assignment, the battlefield morphs on you, and each objective needs to be dealt with in turn.



##### 7\. Contested Ground

###### Mission Overview

Contested ground simulates a large-scale battle as your forces probe forward into enemy lines. Built around the major push structure, it follows a similar flow without the fixed assets to secure. Not so much a stand-up fight as an escalating battle that draws more enemy forces in, Contested Ground takes the more easily managed waves of Warzone and places more pressure on the player to manage an expanding battle.



###### Mission Length

Moderate to long, depending on your decisions.



###### Lance Composition Advisory

Be ready for a slugfest here, as you will need to be able to methodically take out foes who routinely get reenforced from behind..



###### Opposition Notes

The opposition is similar to that faced in Major Push, though without the defensive installations.



###### Designer Thoughts

Like Major Push, this misison simulates a large battle, where you nd allied forces are driving into the opposing force's main line. The enemy is structured in defensive lines, so that getting in behind one can be quite dangerous as others come up to reinforce. This mission test both endurance and your ability to balance risky tactics with the need to fight through a long battle.



##### 8\. Reconnaissance

###### Mission Overview

This mission sets you on the battlefield alone, with a significant weight cap, and sets you to survey the area. You must navigate through a series of waypoints, while hunted by different enemy lances. Speed is your friend, and fighting can only be an option of last resort. This mission sounds easy, but the complexities of needing to manage waypoints and a closing net make for a very difficult challenge.



###### Mission Length

Moderate.



###### Lance Composition Advisory

You have a single ‘Mech that mut balance speed, firepower and armour perfectly. ECM is extremely valuable for this mission type.



###### Opposition Notes

Light ‘Mechs and tanks do not seem like much to face, but death by a thousand cuts is still death.



###### Designer Thoughts

This was the mission I had the most fun with. On your own, behind enemy lines, with more and more foes appearing, the need for precision decision making and the way priorities change based on no more than mounting pressure was a fun challenge to face. This seems, on the surface like an easy assignment, though it is anything but, and is one of the more challenging missions to attempt.



##### 9\. Scout Hunt

###### Mission Overview

In scout hunt, your lance moves onto the map in a dispersed fashion and must come together at two separate points. From there you must hunt down and destroy a fast-moving enemy scout before it reaches its evac zone and escapes.



###### Mission Length

Short

&#x20;

###### Lance Composition Advisory

You have 2 Mechs against 1, but where it is, and if you can catch it when you find it make for careful Mech selection.



###### Opposition Notes

A single enemy Mech is no a threat physically, but bringing it down is more of a challenge than you might think on a map where you can see very little.



###### Designer Thoughts

This mission, like Reconnaissance, is short and fast, where fighting is only part of the effort. Finding your foes, and then taking them out before they get away is vital, and sitting on their evac and simply waiting is a failing strategy. Tis mission agains tests how you maximise limited assets to achieve a goal on a map you can't see a lot of.



##### 10\. Prevent the Landing

###### Mission Overview

This mission inverts the tactical problem of the Planetary Landing mission and places you in the position of having to stop an enemy landing. Both sides have fewer combat forces, but the convoy numbers remain the same, meaning you have to deal with defending forces, as well as convoys heading out all over the map.



###### Mission Length

Moderate



###### Lance Composition Advisory

Speed and firepower will be essential, as will multiple player lances.



###### Opposition Notes

The defence is heavy set, thought the convoys are not.



###### Designer Thoughts

Another mission where you need to split priorities between protecting yourself while completing multiple objectives, this mission really pushes you to cover a lot of ground so you don't fail the mission. The enemy defence is tenacious, and with convoys spawning and running in every direction, you need a lot of careful through as to which Mechs hold the centre and which you dispatch outwards.



##### 11\. Prevent the Evacuation

###### Mission Overview

This mission inverts the tactical problem of the Planetary Evacuation mission and places you in the position of having to stop an enemy evacuation. Both sides have fewer combat forces, but the convoys remain the same, meaning you have to deal with defending forces, as well as convoys coming in from all over the map.



###### Mission Length

Long



###### Lance Composition Advisory

Speed and firepower will be essential, as will multiple player lances.



###### Opposition Notes

The defence is heavy set, thought the convoys are not.



###### Designer Thoughts

Not quite as hard as Prevent the Landing, as your enemy is, in some ways, coming to you, this mission can lull you into a false sense of confidence. If the lead element of a convoy arrives quickly and you did not take it out short of its lift off area, while the tail is miles way, you will fail the mission if you don't kill the tale within 4 rounds as well.



##### 12\. The Vice

###### Mission Overview

The terrible problem of being left behind and having to wait for pick up is the theme of this mission. You and your ally are on your own with enemy forces closing in from all sides. You have to survive long enough to evacuate.



###### Mission Length

Moderate



###### Lance Composition Advisory

Big enough to live is not fast enough to escape, so careful balance is required here.



###### Opposition Notes

Heavy and medium forces are coming at you here.



###### Designer Thoughts

Caught in the vice is the feeling and reality of this mission as foes close from every direction, while you and you allies desperately try to run out the clock. This is a fun mission, where a key to success exists, but it does come at a cost, as no matter which way you turn, you will face fearsome enemy fire.



##### 13\. By the Sword

###### Mission Overview

Mercenary life is unpredictable, and when rumour, or failures in allied intel point fingers at you, then you are dumped right in the middle of it. Spinning the three-way battle and switch-assassinate processes, your lance finds itself betrayed and very alone between two now hostile forces.



###### Mission Length

Moderate



###### Lance Composition Advisory

Heavy firepower and armour should get you through here, or the ability to run like the dickens can help too.



###### Opposition Notes

Heavy enemy and more numerous medium former allies will make for an interesting mix.



###### Designer Thoughts

Being betrayed on the field due to circumstance greatly changes the dynamic of this mission. Do you give into anger and smash your former allies, or focus on the real enemy and then leg it for the evac to sort things out post-mission. The former is easier, but the latter ensures a better payout.
